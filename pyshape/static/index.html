Load geometry

STL (native), or OBJ, PLY, OFF, GLB, 3MF or DAE (read by trimesh).

Input: units of the coordinates in the file. Output: unit of lengths in the results (areas in its square, volumes in its cube, inertia per unit density in its fifth power). As-is keeps the file's units and disables the output choice.

PCA directions are not unique for highly symmetric shapes (e.g. cubes); the minimum-volume box is orientation-independent but slower.
